摘要 Abstract
The Qigu salt pan wetland is located in the Qigu District, Tainan City, which originally belonged to the salt industry. After being completely abandoned in 2002, due to its rich ecological environment, it was announced as a national-level important wetland in 2007. Most of the Qigu salt pans are abandoned salt pans. The large wetlands produced by these abandoned salt pans can be used as alternative habitats for a variety of biological habitats, foraging and reproduction. However, in order to avoid being affected by the tide when salt making in the early years, most of the salt pans were built as closed designs. After the salt pans were completely abandoned, due to neglect of management and use, in non-flood period, the water level in the salt pans was often too low because of poor rainfall, resulting in poor water quality and excessive salinity in the salt pans. These problems will limit the possibility of biological use of these anthropogenic habitats.
七股鹽田濕地位於台南市七股區，原屬鹽業用地。2002年全面廢曬後，因其豐富生態環境，於2007年被評定為國家級重要濕地。濕地內多數為廢曬鹽田，這些大片人工濕地可作為多種生物的替代性棲地、覓食與繁殖場所。然而，早期為避免潮汐影響，鹽田多採封閉式設計。廢曬後因疏於管理，非汛期缺乏降雨時，鹽田水位常過低，導致水質不佳、鹽度過高，進而限制生物利用這些人為棲地的可能性。
Therefore, this study hoped to satisfy the two needs of improving the quality of the salt pan habitat and flood prevention in the surrounding villages through different water gate operation strategies. At the same time, this study would also discuss the impact of engineering methods to change the condition of the habitat on the operation of the water gate. In this study, the water inflow operation was designed into 2 scenarios, and the draining operation was divided into 6 scenarios according to the tidal relationship of each month. The rainfall scenarios with different return periods were used to assess the impact of water inflow operation on the flood detention function of salt pans. After determining the water gate operation strategy, this study would design culvert dredging, mound removal, and elevation change, these three engineering methods to improve habitat.
因此，本研究希望透過不同的水門操作策略，同時滿足「提升鹽田棲地品質」與「周遭村落防洪」兩項需求，並探討工程方法改變棲地現狀對水門操作的影響。引水操作設計為2種情境，排水操作依各月潮汐關係分為6種狀況；不同重現期降雨情境則用以評估引水操作對鹽田滯洪功能的影響。在確定水門操作策略後，再設計涵洞疏通、擋土移除、改變高程等三種工程方法進行棲地改善。
The water inflow operation simulation results showed that if the detention pond next to the salt pan was also included in the scope of the water inflow operation, the state of continuous water inflow can be achieved. The water in the salt pan would follow the tidal fluctuations outside the water gate to make the salt pan become a kind of tidal wetland. From the results of the draining scenario, it was found that January was the easiest month to drain, and August was the most difficult month. Since rainfall events mostly occur in summer, so this study suggested that as long as there was no rainfall event in summer, draining operations should be carried out to keep the salt pan water level at a low water level. From the rainfall simulation, it was found that the long duration water inflow operation did not increase the risk of flooding in the surrounding villages. The results of the engineering scenario showed that the culvert dredging could make the water circulation in the salt pan a little smoother. Remove the mound in the channel, no matter in any scenario, there was no change in the water depth of salt pan. Changing the elevation could not only increase the flood detention capacity of the salt pan, but also significantly increased the water area in the salt pan.
引水操作模擬結果顯示，若將鹽田旁蓄水池一併納入引水範圍，可達成持續常態引水，鹽田內水位隨水門外潮汐波動，形成類感潮濕地。排水情境結果顯示，1月最易排水、8月最難；由於降雨多發生於夏季，建議夏季只要無降雨事件即進行排水，使鹽田維持低水位。降雨模擬發現，長時間引水操作並未增加周遭村落淹水風險。工程情境結果則顯示，涵洞疏通能讓水循環稍加順暢；移除渠道擋土對水深幾乎無影響；改變高程不僅可增加滯洪空間，亦能明顯提升鹽田水域面積。

Chapter 1.1 Background
The East Asian-Australasian Flyway has the most abundant of migratory bird species in the world, including many threatened bird species. In recent years, the loss of habitat due to climate change and human factors has gradually reduced the number of bird populations on the East Asian-Australasian Flyway. As an important stop-over site on the flyway, Taiwan is visited and used by a large number of migratory birds every autumn and winter, including the black-faced spoonbill which is classified as endangered by the IUCN. Globe more than 50% of spoonbills winter regularly on the southwestern coast of Taiwan, and the Qigu District in Tainan has the largest number.
東亞–澳洲遷飛路線擁有全球最豐富的候鳥物種，包含多種受脅鳥類。近年因氣候變遷與人為因素造成棲地喪失，遷飛路線上的鳥類族群逐漸減少。台灣作為重要停歇地，每年秋冬有大量候鳥利用，其中包括被IUCN列為瀕危的黑面琵鷺。全球超過50%的黑面琵鷺固定於台灣西南沿海度冬，而台南七股區擁有最大數量。
In order to avoid the influence of tides when salt making in the early years, most of the salt pans were planned as closed designs, and builded the water conservancy facilities, such as culverts, water gates, channels, to take water from the sea to the salt pan. After stop salt making, most of these water conservancy facilities have lost their functions because of lack management and use. Frequent rainfall during the flood period of spring and summer often causes the water level in the salt pan to be high. Due to problems such as culvert collapse and channel siltation, the water in the salt pan cannot be efficiently drained. The low detention storage sometimes causes flooding in the surrounding village during the rainfall. During non-flood period in autumn and winter, due to lack of rainfall and no natural hydrological connection, most of the salt pans are dry. These situation would lead to problems such as poor water quality and high salinity in the salt pans, and these problems might limit the bioavailability.
早期為避免潮汐影響，鹽田多採封閉式設計，並設置涵洞、水門、渠道等水利設施自海上引水。停曬後，這些設施因疏於管理而功能喪失。春夏汛期頻繁降雨使鹽田水位偏高，加上涵洞塌陷、渠道淤積，排水效率低落，滯洪空間不足，有時造成周邊村落淹水。秋冬非汛期則因缺乏降雨與自然水文連結，多數鹽田乾涸，導致水質惡化、鹽度升高，進而限制生物可利用性。
Chapter 1.2 Motivation and Purpose
In recent years, many studies used water gate operation to control the water level in the salt pan, and the purpose were improve quality of the habitat or increase the detention storage through artificial method. This study selected the Taiqu salt pan in the Qigu as the study area. Using the Physiographic drainage-inundation Model (PHD Model) to simulate the current situation in different operating scenarios according to the needs of drainage and drainage. At the same time assessed the impact of water inflow operations on the flood prevention demand of nearby villages. Then combined the settings of different engineering scenarios such as culvert dredging, mound removed, changing elevations, to discuss the impact of different engineering methods on water inflow operation, drainage operation, and the flood detention function of Taiqu salt pans. The aim of this study have two: (1) Integrate the results of the current operation scenario condition and establish a long-term watergate operation strategy ; (2) Discuss the impact of using engineering methods to change habitats on different water gate operation demand. Find a balance between flood prevention and improvement of habitat quality, and provide a reference for future government or related units when doing related improvement engineering projects.
近年已有多項研究透過水門操作控制鹽田水位，以提升棲地品質或增加滯洪容量。本研究選定七股台區鹽田為研究區，應用地文性淹排水模式（PHD Model）模擬不同操作情境下的現況，並評估引水操作對鄰近村落防洪需求的影響。進一步結合涵洞疏通、擋土移除、改變高程等工程情境，探討工程方法對引水、排水及滯洪功能的影響。研究目標有二：（1）整合現況操作情境結果，建立長期水門操作策略；（2）探討工程改變棲地對不同水門操作需求的影響，尋求防洪與棲地品質提升之間的平衡，供未來政府或相關單位辦理改善工程時參考。
Chapter 2.2 Environmental Factors
Ma et al. (2010) divided the environmental factors affecting waterbirds into nine items including water depth, water level fluctuations, vegetation, salinity, landforms, food supply, wetland area, wetland connectivity, and others. The survey results showed that different water depth environments can attract different species of birds to use. Water level fluctuations caused by tides or seasons can increase habitat diversity. Habitat too high salinity will reduce the willingness of birds to use. Abundant food supply can quickly attract a large number of waterbirds to use the habitat.
Ma 等人（2010）將影響水鳥的環境因子歸納為九項：水深、水位波動、植被、鹽度、地形、食物供給、濕地面積、濕地連結性等。調查顯示，不同水深環境可吸引不同鳥種利用；潮汐或季節造成的水位波動能增加棲地多樣性；過高鹽度會降低鳥類利用意願；豐富的食物供給則可迅速吸引大量水鳥。
Chapter 2.3 Impact of Gate Operation
Rocha et al. (2016) conducted a water gate operation experiment in a salt pan fish pond in the Tagus estuary region of Portugal. This study opened the watergate to let water flow into the pond during non-flood period and keeps the pond at a high water level for up to seven months. During this period, the abundance of benthic organisms continued to increase. When the water gate was opened to drain, waterbirds from the nearby estuary quickly gathered for food (about 6% of total estuary waterbirds). This experiment shows that habitat abundance can be increased by water gate operation.
Rocha 等人（2016）在葡萄牙塔霍河口鹽田魚塭進行水門操作實驗。非汛期開啟水門引水並維持高水位長達七個月，期間底棲生物豐度持續上升；排水後鄰近河口約6%的水鳥迅速聚集覓食。此實驗證明水門操作可增加棲地豐度。
Kuo and Wang (2018) used water inflow operation in non-flood period at the Chiayi Budai salt pan wetland. Operation duration about 5.5 hours resulted in about 12,600 cubic meters of water flowing into the salt field. The increase in water content and bird numbers indicated effective improvement of climate regulation and biodiversity ecosystem services.
郭與王（2018）於非汛期對嘉義布袋鹽田進行引水操作約5.5小時，約12,600立方公尺的水流入鹽田。棲地含水量與鳥類數量增加，顯示可有效提升氣候調節與生物多樣性兩項生態系統服務。
Chapter 2.4 PHD Model
The Physiographic Drainage-inundation Model (PHD Model) is a physical process-based quasi two-dimensional model developed by Chen et al. (2007). It uses Cunge’s quasi two-dimensional flow theory and is combined with GIS to perform calculations according to the geographic characteristics of the study area.
地文性淹排水模式（PHD Model）是由陳等人（2007）發展的物理過程式準二維模式，採用 Cunge 準二維流理論，並結合地理資訊系統依研究區地理特性進行計算。
Chapter 3 Research Area and Methods
The research area was Taiqu Salt Pan in Yancheng Li, Qigu District, Tainan City. Operable water gates were restricted to No.3 and No.18. Water inflow was designed into 2 scenarios; draining into 6 scenarios by monthly tide; rainfall scenarios of different return periods assessed flood detention impact. Three engineering methods were then applied: culvert dredging, mound removal, and elevation change.
研究區為台南市七股區鹽埕里台區鹽田。可操作水門為 No.3 與 No.18。引水操作設計為2種情境，排水依各月潮汐分為6種；不同重現期降雨用以評估滯洪影響。其後再設計涵洞疏通、擋土移除、改變高程三種工程方法。
Chapter 4 Key Simulation Results
Including the adjacent detention pond enabled continuous water inflow, making the salt pan behave like a tidal wetland. January was easiest to drain, August hardest; summer draining is recommended when no rainfall occurs. Long-duration inflow did not increase village flooding risk. Culvert dredging slightly improved circulation; mound removal had almost no effect on water depth; elevation change increased both flood detention capacity and water area.
將旁側蓄水池納入引水可達成持續引水，鹽田形成類感潮濕地。1月最易排水、8月最難；建議夏季無雨時排水維持低水位。長時間引水未增加村落淹水風險。涵洞疏通略改善循環；擋土移除幾乎無水深變化；改變高程可同時增加滯洪空間與水域面積。
Chapter 5 Conclusion and Suggestion
This study hoped to provide a reference for future related research on the use of water gate operations or engineering methods to improve the quality of habitats. All scenarios were simulated using the physiographic drainage-inundation model to observe water depth changes in the study area.
本研究希望為未來利用水門操作或工程手段提升鹽田棲地品質的相關研究提供參考。上述情境皆應用地文性淹排水模式進行模擬，以觀察研究區域內的水深變化。
