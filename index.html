Chapter 1 Introduction　第1章 緒論
1.1 Background　背景
The East Asian-Australasian Flyway has the most abundant of migratory bird species in the world, including many threatened bird species. In recent years, the loss of habitat due to climate change and human factors has gradually reduced the number of bird populations. Taiwan is visited by a large number of migratory birds every autumn and winter, including the black-faced spoonbill (IUCN endangered). Globe more than 50% of spoonbills winter regularly on the southwestern coast of Taiwan, and the Qigu District in Tainan has the largest number.
東亞–澳洲遷飛路線擁有全球最豐富的候鳥物種。近年因氣候變遷與人為因素造成棲地喪失，鳥類族群逐漸減少。台灣每年秋冬有大量候鳥利用，包括被IUCN列為瀕危的黑面琵鷺。全球超過50%的黑面琵鷺固定於台灣西南沿海度冬，台南七股區擁有最大數量。
The Qigu Salt pan Wetland is in the Qigu District of Tainan City. Originally the largest salt pan in Taiwan (1976 hectares). History traces back to the Ming and Zheng Dynasties; developed during Japanese occupation; became Taiwan Salt Factory after WWII; closed in May 2002. Taiwan's 338-year salt industry entered history.
七股鹽田濕地位於台南市七股區，原為台灣最大鹽田（1976公頃）。歷史可追溯至明鄭時期，日治時期開發，戰後為台灣製鹽總廠，2002年5月全面停曬。台灣338年鹽業歷史至此結束。
It provides habitat for Black-faced Spoonbills, Dunlin, Striated Heron, Osprey, etc. Announced as national important wetland in 2007 by Taijiang National Park — core conservation wetland of the southwest coast and the most important black-faced spoonbill overwintering habitat in Taiwan.
為黑面琵鷺、濱鷸、綠鷺、魚鷹等提供棲地。2007年由台江國家公園公告為國家級重要濕地，定位為西南沿海核心保育濕地及黑面琵鷺最重要度冬棲地。
Early closed designs with culverts, water gates, channels. After abandonment, facilities lost function. Flood-period high water + siltation causes village flooding risk; non-flood period dry pans lead to poor water quality and high salinity, limiting bioavailability.
早期封閉式設計設有涵洞、水門、渠道。廢曬後設施失能。汛期水位高且淤積造成村落淹水風險；非汛期乾涸導致水質不佳、鹽度升高，限制生物可利用性。
1.2 Motivation and Purpose　研究動機與目的
This study selected Taiqu salt pan, used PHD Model to simulate operating scenarios, assessed inflow impact on village flood prevention, and combined engineering scenarios (culvert dredging, mound removal, elevation change). Aims: (1) establish long-term watergate operation strategy; (2) find balance between flood prevention and habitat quality improvement.
本研究選定台區鹽田，以PHD模式模擬操作情境，評估引水對村落防洪影響，並結合涵洞疏通、擋土移除、改變高程等工程情境。目標：（1）建立長期水門操作策略；（2）尋求防洪與棲地品質提升的平衡。

Chapter 2 Literature Review　第2章 文獻回顧
2.1 Wetland Definition　濕地定義
The Ramsar Convention defines wetlands as areas of marsh, fen, peatland or water, whether natural or artificial, permanent or temporary, with water that is static or flowing, fresh, brackish or salt, including marine water not exceeding six meters at low tide (Ramsar Convention, 1971). Taiwan's Wetland Conservation Law (2013) has a similar definition including sea areas not exceeding 6 m at lowest tide.
《拉姆薩公約》定義濕地為天然或人工、永久或暫時、靜止或流動、淡水或微鹹或鹹水的沼澤、泥炭地或水域，包括低潮時水深不超過六公尺的海域。台灣《濕地保育法》（2013）定義相近，包含最低潮時水深不超過六公尺之海域。
2.2 Environmental Factors　環境因子
Wetlands cover ~3% of world area but provide nearly 40% of ecosystem services. Global wetland area fell ~35% from 1970–2015. Ma et al. (2010) listed nine factors affecting waterbirds: water depth, water level fluctuations, vegetation, salinity, landforms, food supply, wetland area, wetland connectivity, and others.
濕地僅占全球約3%面積，卻提供近40%生態系統服務。1970–2015年全球濕地面積減少約35%。Ma等人（2010）列出影響水鳥的九項因子：水深、水位波動、植被、鹽度、地形、食物供給、濕地面積、濕地連結性等。
David et al. (2016): arthropod abundance recovered quickly after restoring tidal action. Hammock et al. (2019): fish stomach fullness roughly doubled as tidal wetland water area increased from 0 to 4.89 km².
David等人（2016）：恢復潮汐作用後節肢動物豐度迅速回升。Hammock等人（2019）：感潮濕地水域面積從0增至4.89 km²時，魚胃飽滿度約增加兩倍。
2.3 Impact of Gate Operation　水門操作影響
Rocha et al. (2016) kept high water for up to seven months in a Tagus estuary salt pan pond; benthic abundance rose continuously; ~6% of estuary waterbirds gathered after drainage. Kuo & Wang (2018) at Budai: ~12,600 m³ inflow in 5.5 h improved climate regulation and biodiversity services. Wang et al. (2020): short operations create diverse depths and salinities without increasing community flood risk.
Rocha等人（2016）維持高水位長達七個月，底棲豐度持續上升，排水後約6%河口水鳥聚集。郭與王（2018）於布袋：5.5小時引入約12,600 m³，提升氣候調節與生物多樣性服務。王等人（2020）：短時間操作可創造多樣水深與鹽度，且不增加社區淹水風險。
2.4 PHD Model Application　PHD模式應用
The Physiographic Drainage-inundation Model (PHD Model) is a physical process-based quasi-2D model by Chen et al. (2007), using Cunge's theory and GIS. Kuo (2015) and Peng (2017) applied it to Budai salt pan for gate-operation and land-use scenarios.
地文性淹排水模式（PHD Model）由陳等人（2007）發展，為物理過程式準二維模式，採用Cunge理論並結合GIS。郭（2015）與彭（2017）曾應用於布袋鹽田進行水門操作與土地利用情境模擬。
Chapter 3 Research Area and Methods　第3章 研究區與方法
3.1 Study Area　研究區
Taiqu Salt Pan in Yancheng Li, Qigu District, Tainan. National important wetland since 2007. Bounded by South Channel (N), Xiashan Stream estuary (S), Highway 61 (E), Qigu Lagoon (W). Four gates: No.1–3 and No.18. Operable gates restricted to No.3 and No.18 (No.1/2 limited by aquaculture).
台南市七股區鹽埕里台區鹽田，2007年納入國家級重要濕地。北以南渠道、南以下山溪河口、東以61號快速道路、西以七股潟湖為界。四座水門中，本研究僅操作No.3與No.18（No.1/2受限於養殖使用）。
Essentially a closed region without rainfall or gate operation. Terrain is the Jianan Coastal Uplift Plain, gentle slope 1/800–1/1000 east to west. Formed by Zengwen River sediment and historical Taijiang Inner Sea silting.
無降雨且不操作水門時屬封閉區域。地屬嘉南沿海隆起平原，由東向西緩降（坡度1/800–1/1000），由曾文溪泥沙與台江內海淤積形成。
3.2 Facilities and Gate Operation　設施與水門操作
Culverts, gates, channels, detention pond present. Culvert 2 (A5) poorly connected due to silt; Culvert 1 under road to detention pond; mound between H and village prevents backflow. Local practice: No.3 for drainage at low tide (flood prevention); No.18 for monthly high-tide inflow (sewer flushing); No.44 (2020) isolates village during heavy rain.
區內有涵洞、水門、渠道與蓄水池。涵洞2（A5）因淤積連通不佳；涵洞1位於通往蓄水池道路下；H與村落間擋土防止回流。實務：No.3於低潮排水防洪；No.18於高潮引水沖洗下水道；No.44（2020）豪雨時隔離村落。
Scenarios: 2 inflow + 6 monthly drainage + rainfall return periods. Engineering: culvert dredging, mound removal, elevation change. All simulated with PHD Model.
情境：2種引水＋6種月別排水＋不同重現期降雨。工程：涵洞疏通、擋土移除、改變高程。皆以PHD模式模擬。
