Chapter 1 Introduction　第1章 緒論
1.1 Background　背景
The East Asian-Australasian Flyway has the most abundant of migratory bird species in the world, including many threatened bird species. In recent years, the loss of habitat due to climate change and human factors has gradually reduced the number of bird populations on the East Asian-Australasian Flyway. As an important stop-over site on the flyway, Taiwan is visited and used by a large number of migratory birds every autumn and winter, including the black-faced spoonbill which is classified as endangered by the IUCN. Globe more than 50% of spoonbills winter regularly on the southwestern coast of Taiwan, and the Qigu District in Tainan has the largest number.
東亞–澳洲遷飛路線擁有全球最豐富的候鳥物種，包含多種受脅鳥類。近年因氣候變遷與人為因素造成棲地喪失，遷飛路線上的鳥類族群逐漸減少。台灣作為重要停歇地，每年秋冬有大量候鳥利用，其中包括被IUCN列為瀕危的黑面琵鷺。全球超過50%的黑面琵鷺固定於台灣西南沿海度冬，而台南七股區擁有最大數量。
The Qigu Salt pan Wetland is in the Qigu District of Tainan City. Originally it was used for salt industry, which was the largest salt pan in Taiwan, with the total area of 1976 hectares. Its history can be traced back to the Ming and Zheng Dynasties, and was developed during the Japanese occupation period. After Second World War, it became a state-owned enterprise "Taiwan Salt Factory" and gradually replaced traditional methods with mechanization. Later, due to the high production costs, meanwhile some of salt pan were planned as industrial zone and protection areas in 2001. Therefore, it was closed by Taiwan Salt Factory in May 2002. Taiwan’s salt industry in 338 years has since entered history.
七股鹽田濕地位於台南市七股區，原為台灣規模最大的鹽田，總面積達1976公頃。其歷史可追溯至明鄭時期，日治時期進一步開發。二次大戰後成為國營「台灣製鹽總廠」，並逐漸以機械化取代傳統製鹽。後因生產成本偏高，部分鹽田於2001年被規劃為工業區或保護區，台灣製鹽總廠遂於2002年5月全面停曬。台灣長達338年的鹽業歷史至此畫下句點。
The Qigu Salt Pan Wetland is a diverse habitat, including sandbars, lagoons, abandoned salt pans, fish pond, mangroves and others. It provides abundant natural resources to variety of precious birds such as Black-faced Spoonbills, Dunlin, Striated Heron, Osprey, etc. The salt industry history of more than 300 years has also given it many cultural buildings and history. Therefore, Qigu Salt Wetland was announced as national important wetland in 2007 by Taijiang National Park, and positioned it as the core conservation wetland of the southwest coast of Taiwan and the most important habitat for the black-faced spoonbill to overwinter in Taiwan. It is planned the three major goals, including protection of biodiversity, participatory management and enhancing environmental education.
七股鹽田濕地包含沙洲、潟湖、廢曬鹽田、魚塭、紅樹林等多樣棲地，為黑面琵鷺、濱鷸、綠鷺、魚鷹等珍貴鳥類提供豐富自然資源。三百多年的鹽業歷史也留下許多文化建築與史蹟。因此，台江國家公園於2007年將七股鹽田濕地公告為國家級重要濕地，定位為台灣西南沿海核心保育濕地及黑面琵鷺最重要的度冬棲地，並規劃生物多樣性保護、參與式管理與環境教育強化三大目標。
In order to avoid the influence of tides when salt making in the early years, most of the salt pans were planned as closed designs, and builded the water conservancy facilities, such as culverts, water gates, channels, to take water from the sea to the salt pan. After stop salt making, most of these water conservancy facilities have lost their functions because of lack management and use. Frequent rainfall during the flood period of spring and summer often causes the water level in the salt pan to be high. Due to problems such as culvert collapse and channel siltation, the water in the salt pan cannot be efficiently drained. The low detention storage sometimes causes flooding in the surrounding village during the rainfall. During non-flood period in autumn and winter, due to lack of rainfall and no natural hydrological connection, most of the salt pans are dry. These situation would lead to problems such as poor water quality and high salinity in the salt pans, and these problems might limit the bioavailability.
早期為避免潮汐影響，鹽田多採封閉式設計，並設置涵洞、水門、渠道等水利設施自海上引水。停曬後，這些設施因疏於管理而功能喪失。春夏汛期頻繁降雨使鹽田水位偏高，加上涵洞塌陷、渠道淤積，排水效率低落，滯洪空間不足，有時造成周邊村落淹水。秋冬非汛期則因缺乏降雨與自然水文連結，多數鹽田乾涸，導致水質惡化、鹽度升高，進而限制生物可利用性。
1.2 Motivation and Purpose　研究動機與目的
In recent years, many studies used water gate operation to control the water level in the salt pan, and the purpose were improve quality of the habitat or increase the detention storage through artificial method. This study selected the Taiqu salt pan in the Qigu as the study area. Using the Physiographic drainage-inundation Model (PHD Model) to simulate the current situation in different operating scenarios according to the needs of drainage and drainage. At the same time assessed the impact of water inflow operations on the flood prevention demand of nearby villages. Then combined the settings of different engineering scenarios such as culvert dredging, mound removed, changing elevations, to discuss the impact of different engineering methods on water inflow operation, drainage operation, and the flood detention function of Taiqu salt pans. The aim of this study have two: (1) Integrate the results of the current operation scenario condition and establish a long-term watergate operation strategy ; (2) Discuss the impact of using engineering methods to change habitats on different water gate operation demand. Find a balance between flood prevention and improvement of habitat quality, and provide a reference for future government or related units when doing related improvement engineering projects.
近年已有多項研究透過水門操作控制鹽田水位，以提升棲地品質或增加滯洪容量。本研究選定七股台區鹽田為研究區，應用地文性淹排水模式（PHD Model）模擬不同操作情境下的現況，並評估引水操作對鄰近村落防洪需求的影響。進一步結合涵洞疏通、擋土移除、改變高程等工程情境，探討工程方法對引水、排水及滯洪功能的影響。研究目標有二：（1）整合現況操作情境結果，建立長期水門操作策略；（2）探討工程改變棲地對不同水門操作需求的影響，尋求防洪與棲地品質提升之間的平衡，供未來政府或相關單位辦理改善工程時參考。

Chapter 3 Research Area and Methods　第3章 研究區與方法
3.1 Study Area　研究區
The research area of this study was Taiqu Salt Pan, located in Yancheng Li, Qigu District, Tainan City, Taiwan. The Taiqu Salt Pan was announced as a part of the national important protection wetland in 2007, surrounded by South Channel to the north, the Xiashan Stream estuary to the south, the 61 highways to the east, and the Qigu Lagoon to the west. There were four water gates in the region (No.1, No.2, No.3, and No.18). In the past, these gates were set up to let seawater from the lagoon inflow to the salt pans for salt making. After abandonment, except for drainage during flood season, the gates are basically not operated additionally.
本研究區為台南市七股區鹽埕里的台區鹽田，2007年被納入國家級重要濕地。北以南渠道、南以下山溪河口、東以61號快速道路、西以七股潟湖為界。區內有四座水門（No.1、No.2、No.3、No.18）。過去用於自潟湖引海水製鹽；廢曬後除汛期排水外，基本上不再額外操作。
圖 3.1 研究區位置 / Figure 3.1 Study area
（原文第15頁截圖已備妥）
This study area was a part of Taiqu Salt Pan, bounded by roads and dikes that were difficult for water to pass. Without rainfall and water gate operation, the salt pans were difficult to exchange water with the outside, so it was basically a closed region. Due to gates No.1 and No.2 being limited to local aquaculture fishery use, this study restricts the operable water gates to No.3 and No.18.
本研究範圍為台區鹽田的一部分，四周以道路與堤防為界，不易透水。在無降雨且不操作水門的情況下，鹽田幾乎無法與外界交換水體，屬封閉區域。因 No.1 與 No.2 受限於地方養殖漁業使用，本研究僅以 No.3 與 No.18 為可操作水門。
圖 3.2 研究區邊界 / Figure 3.2 Study area boundary
（原文第16頁截圖已備妥）
The study area is located on the southwest coast of Taiwan, belonging to the Jianan Coastal Uplift Plain. The overall terrain is gentle; the slope decreases from east to west between 1/800 and 1/1000. In early years, the Zengwen River carried large amounts of sediment to the estuary forming the Taijiang Inner Sea. Multiple diversions of the Zengwen River caused continued westward accumulation of sediment, silting up the Inner Sea and making the Zengwen estuary the westernmost point of Taiwan’s main island. The coastal area of Qigu evolved into the present offshore bar landscape.
研究區位於台灣西南沿海的七股區，屬嘉南沿海隆起平原，地勢平緩，由東向西緩降，坡度約1/800至1/1000。早期曾文溪攜帶大量泥沙至河口形成台江內海；因多次改道，泥沙持續向西堆積，內海逐漸淤積，使曾文溪口成為台灣本島最西端，七股沿海也演變成今日的外傘頂洲地貌。
3.2 Facilities and Gate Operation　設施與水門操作
There were culverts, water gates, channels and a detention pond in the study area. Culvert 2 at salt pan A5 has poor water connectivity due to sediment deposition. Culvert 1 in the channel in front of A1 was built under the road between the salt pan and the detention pond; above it were two operable boards kept open normally. There was a mound in the channel between salt pan H and the village to prevent water from the salt pan flowing back to the village during heavy rainfall.
研究區內有涵洞、水門、渠道與蓄水池。位於A5的涵洞2因泥沙淤積，水力連通性不佳。A1前方渠道的涵洞1建於鹽田與蓄水池之間道路下方，上方有兩扇可操作閘板，平時保持開啟。H鹽田與村落之間的渠道有擋土，防止豪雨時鹽田水回流至村落。
圖 3.10 研究區內設施 / Figure 3.10 The facilities in study area
（原文第25–26頁截圖已備妥）
According to interviews with local residents, water gates were operated for two purposes. One was drainage of gate No.3 for flood prevention: when the salt pan water level was too high, the operator opened No.3 at the lowest tide to drain water into Qigu Lagoon. Another was inflow of gate No.18 for village sewer flushing (one or twice a month at high tide). The No.44 gate (built April 2020) prevents water from the western detention pond pouring back into the village during heavy rain; when heavy rain occurs, No.44 is closed and pumps at No.18 and No.3 operate until the rain ends.
根據居民訪談，水門操作有兩個目的：一是No.3水門的排水防洪——水位過高時於最低潮開啟，將水排入七股潟湖；二是No.18水門的引水，用於村落下水道沖洗（每月一至兩次於高潮開啟）。2020年4月新建的No.44水門用於防止西側蓄水池豪雨時倒灌村落；豪雨時會關閉No.44並開啟抽水機直至雨停。
情境設計概述　Scenario Design
Water inflow operation was designed into 2 scenarios; draining operation was divided into 6 scenarios according to the tidal relationship of each month. Rainfall scenarios with different return periods assessed the impact of inflow on flood detention. After determining the gate operation strategy, three engineering methods were designed: culvert dredging, mound removal, and elevation change.
引水操作設計為2種情境，排水操作依各月潮汐關係分為6種狀況；不同重現期降雨情境用以評估引水對滯洪功能的影響。確定水門操作策略後，再設計涵洞疏通、擋土移除、改變高程三種工程方法改善棲地。
The Physiographic Drainage-Inundation Model (PHD Model) is a physical process-based quasi two-dimensional model using Cunge’s theory, combined with GIS according to the geographic characteristics of the study area. Frequency analysis of rainfall and design hyetographs supported the rainfall scenarios.
地文性淹排水模式（PHD Model）為物理過程式準二維模式，採用Cunge準二維流理論，並結合GIS依研究區地理特性計算。研究中亦進行降雨頻率分析與設計雨型，以支撐降雨情境模擬。
