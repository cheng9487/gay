Chapter 1 Introduction　第1章 緒論
1.1 Background　背景
The East Asian-Australasian Flyway has the most abundant migratory bird species in the world, including many threatened species. In recent years, habitat loss due to climate change and human factors has gradually reduced bird populations along this flyway. Taiwan is an important stop-over site, visited by large numbers of migratory birds every autumn and winter (Vickery et al., 2014; Yong et al., 2015). Among them is the black-faced spoonbill, classified as endangered by the IUCN. More than 50% of the global population winters regularly on the southwestern coast of Taiwan, with the largest numbers occurring in the Qigu District of Tainan (Taiwan Wild Bird Federation, 2020).
東亞–澳洲遷飛路線擁有全球最豐富的候鳥物種，包括許多受脅物種。近年來，氣候變遷與人為因素造成的棲地喪失，使此遷飛路線的鳥類族群逐漸減少。台灣是重要的停歇地，每年秋冬有大量候鳥造訪（Vickery et al., 2014; Yong et al., 2015）。其中黑面琵鷺被IUCN列為瀕危物種，全球超過50%的族群定期於台灣西南沿海度冬，以台南七股區數量最多（台灣野鳥協會，2020）。
The Qigu Salt Pan Wetland is located in the Qigu District of Tainan City. It was originally used for the salt industry and was the largest salt pan in Taiwan, with a total area of 1,976 hectares. Its history can be traced back to the Ming and Zheng Dynasties and was further developed during the Japanese occupation period. After the Second World War it became a state-owned enterprise, the “Taiwan Salt Factory,” and gradually replaced traditional methods with mechanization. Due to high production costs and the planning of some areas as industrial zones and protection areas in 2001, the factory closed the salt pans in May 2002. Taiwan’s salt industry of 338 years thus entered history (Taijiang National Park, 2020).
七股鹽田濕地位於台南市七股區，原為鹽業用地，曾是台灣最大的鹽田，總面積1,976公頃。其歷史可追溯至明鄭時期，日治時期進一步開發。二次大戰後成為國營事業「台鹽」，並逐漸以機械化取代傳統方法。由於生產成本高，且部分鹽田於2001年被劃為工業區與保護區，台鹽於2002年5月全面停曬。台灣延續338年的鹽業自此走入歷史（台江國家公園，2020）。
The Qigu Salt Pan Wetland is a diverse habitat that includes sandbars, lagoons, abandoned salt pans, fish ponds, mangroves and others. It provides abundant natural resources for many precious birds such as Black-faced Spoonbills, Dunlin, Striated Heron and Osprey. The more than 300 years of salt-industry history have also left many cultural buildings and historical remains. Therefore the Qigu Salt Wetland was announced as a national important wetland in 2007 by Taijiang National Park and was positioned as the core conservation wetland of the southwestern coast of Taiwan and the most important overwintering habitat for the black-faced spoonbill in Taiwan. Three major goals were set: protection of biodiversity, participatory management, and enhancement of environmental education, in order to achieve wetland conservation and sustainable utilization (Taijiang National Park, 2020).
七股鹽田濕地是多樣化的棲地，包含沙洲、潟湖、廢曬鹽田、魚塭、紅樹林等。它為黑面琵鷺、濱鷸、綠鷺、魚鷹等珍貴鳥類提供豐富自然資源。超過300年的鹽業歷史也留下許多文化建築與遺跡。因此，七股鹽田濕地於2007年由台江國家公園公告為國家級重要濕地，定位為台灣西南沿海核心保育濕地及黑面琵鷺最重要的度冬棲地，並訂定生物多樣性保護、參與式管理與環境教育提升三大目標，以達成濕地保育與永續利用（台江國家公園，2020）。
The large wetlands produced by the abandoned salt pans can serve not only as alternative habitats for waterbirds to inhabit, forage and breed, but also provide flood detention for the surrounding villages (Dias et al., 2014; Li, 2020). In the early years of salt production, most salt pans were planned as closed designs with water-conservancy facilities (culverts, water gates, channels) to take water from the sea while avoiding tidal influence. After salt production stopped, most of these facilities lost their functions because of lack of management. Frequent rainfall in the flood season often causes high water levels in the salt pans; combined with culvert collapse and channel siltation, water cannot be drained efficiently, and the limited detention storage sometimes causes flooding in surrounding villages (Liao, 2019). During the non-flood period in autumn and winter, lack of rainfall and the absence of natural hydrological connection leave most salt pans dry, leading to poor water quality and high salinity that limit bioavailability (Salim, 2020).
廢曬鹽田形成的大片濕地不僅可作為水鳥棲息、覓食與繁殖的替代棲地，也能為周邊村落提供滯洪功能（Dias et al., 2014; Li, 2020）。早期曬鹽為避免潮汐影響，多數鹽田採封閉式設計，並設置涵洞、水門、渠道等水利設施取水。停曬後因疏於管理，多數設施失能。汛期頻繁降雨常使鹽田水位偏高，加上涵洞坍塌與渠道淤積，排水效率低，有限的滯洪容量有時造成周邊村落淹水（Liao, 2019）。非汛期（秋冬）則因缺乏降雨與自然水文連結，多數鹽田乾涸，導致水質不佳、鹽度過高，限制生物可利用性（Salim, 2020）。
1.2 Motivation and Purpose　研究動機與目的
In recent years many studies have used water-gate operation to control water levels in salt pans, aiming to improve habitat quality or increase detention storage by artificial means (Kuo and Wang, 2018; Wang and Kuo, 2020; Liao, 2019). This study selected the Taiqu salt pan in Qigu as the study area. The Physiographic Drainage-Inundation Model (PHD Model) was used to simulate current conditions under different operating scenarios according to the needs of inflow and drainage, and to assess the impact of water-inflow operations on the flood-prevention demand of nearby villages. Different engineering scenarios (culvert dredging, mound removal, elevation change) were then combined to examine their influence on inflow operation, drainage operation, and the flood-detention function of the Taiqu salt pans. The aims of this study are two-fold: (1) integrate the results of the current operation scenarios and establish a long-term water-gate operation strategy; (2) discuss the impact of engineering methods that change habitats on different water-gate operation demands, seeking a balance between flood prevention and habitat-quality improvement, and providing a reference for future government or related units undertaking improvement projects.
近年許多研究利用水門操作控制鹽田水位，目的在於以人為方式改善棲地品質或增加滯洪容量（Kuo and Wang, 2018; Wang and Kuo, 2020; Liao, 2019）。本研究以七股台區鹽田為研究區，應用地文性淹排水模式（PHD Model）依引水與排水需求模擬不同操作情境的現況，並評估引水操作對鄰近村落防洪需求的影響。接著結合涵洞疏通、擋土移除、改變高程等不同工程情境，探討工程方法對引水操作、排水操作及台區鹽田滯洪功能的影響。本研究目的有二：（1）整合現況操作情境結果，建立長期水門操作策略；（2）探討改變棲地的工程方法對不同水門操作需求的影響，尋求防洪與棲地品質提升之間的平衡，並提供未來政府或相關單位進行改善工程時的參考。

Chapter 2 Literature Review　第2章 文獻回顧
2.1 Wetland Definition　濕地定義
The Ramsar Convention (Convention on Wetlands of International Importance Especially as Waterfowl Habitat) defines wetlands broadly in Article 1 as “areas of marsh, fen, peatland or water, whether natural or artificial, permanent or temporary, with water that is static or flowing, fresh, brackish or salt, including areas of marine water the depth of which at low tide does not exceed six metres” (Ramsar Convention, 1971). Taiwan’s Wetland Conservation Law (2013), Chapter 1 Article 4, adopts a similar definition and classifies wetlands into international, national and local levels of importance.
《拉姆薩公約》（特別是作為水禽棲息地的國際重要濕地公約）第1條將濕地廣義定義為「天然或人工、永久或暫時、靜止或流動、淡水、微鹹或鹹水的沼澤、泥炭地或水域，包括低潮時水深不超過六公尺的海域」（Ramsar Convention, 1971）。台灣《濕地保育法》（2013）第1章第4條採用相近定義，並將濕地分為國際級、國家級與地方級重要濕地。
2.2 Environmental Factors Affecting Organisms in Wetlands　影響濕地生物的環境因子
Although wetlands occupy only about 3% of the Earth’s land surface, they provide approximately 40% of global ecosystem services. Between 1970 and 2015 wetland area declined by about 35%. Ma et al. (2010) identified key environmental factors that influence the use of wetlands by organisms: water depth, water-level fluctuations, vegetation, salinity, landform, food supply, wetland area and wetland connectivity. Different water-depth environments attract different bird species; tidal or seasonal water-level fluctuations increase habitat diversity; excessively high salinity reduces bird willingness to use a site; and abundant food can quickly attract large numbers of waterbirds.
濕地雖僅占地球陸域約3%，卻提供全球約40%的生態系統服務。1970至2015年間濕地面積減少約35%。Ma等人（2010）指出影響生物利用濕地的關鍵環境因子：水深、水位波動、植被、鹽度、地形、食物供給、濕地面積與濕地連結性。不同水深環境吸引不同鳥種；潮汐或季節性水位波動可增加棲地多樣性；過高鹽度會降低鳥類利用意願；豐富的食物則能快速吸引大量水鳥。
David et al. (2016) sampled arthropods from 1998 to 2013 across 87 intertidal wetlands in 13 estuaries on the west coast of North America. They found that arthropod abundance quickly reached average levels after tidal action was restored in some wetlands, indicating that tides can rapidly restore certain components of the wetland ecosystem while other components require longer recovery times. Hammock et al. (2019) quantified stomach fullness of fish in the San Francisco estuary and found that fullness approximately doubled as tidal-wetland water area increased from the smallest to the largest (4.89 km²), with 60% of the diet being juvenile fish and 40% plankton. Curtis Roegner et al. (2021) developed a Fish Habitat Opportunity Index for Columbia River tidal wetlands and showed that both the volume of water inundating a wetland and the duration of inundation are positively related to ecological benefits for juvenile fish.
David等人（2016）於北美西岸13個河口87處潮間帶濕地進行1998–2013年節肢動物採樣，發現部分濕地在恢復潮汐作用後，節肢動物豐度迅速達到平均水準，顯示潮汐可快速恢復濕地生態系的部分組成，其他組成則需更長時間。Hammock等人（2019）量化舊金山河口魚類胃飽滿度，發現潮汐濕地水域面積從最小增至最大（4.89 km²）時，飽滿度約增加一倍，其中60%為幼魚、40%為浮游生物。Curtis Roegner等人（2021）為哥倫比亞河潮汐濕地發展魚類棲地機會指數，顯示淹沒濕地的水量與淹沒時間皆與幼魚的生態效益呈正相關。
2.3 Impact of Gate Operation on Habitat　水門操作對棲地的影響
Water gates, dams, weirs and dikes built for resource development, flood prevention and irrigation cause significant changes in hydrology and landform (Thoms et al., 2005). Such changes readily affect benthic and aquatic organisms as well as the species that feed on them (Bunn et al., 2002), and can reduce available habitat and biodiversity (Kingsford et al., 2016). Appropriate use of these facilities is therefore an important contemporary issue.
為資源開發、防洪與灌溉而興建的水門、水壩、堰與堤防，會顯著改變水文與地形（Thoms et al., 2005）。此類變化容易影響底棲與水生生物及其捕食者（Bunn et al., 2002），並可能減少可用棲地與生物多樣性（Kingsford et al., 2016）。如何適當使用這些設施因此成為當前重要議題。
Rocha et al. (2016) conducted a water-gate experiment in a salt-pan fish pond in the Tagus estuary, Portugal. They maintained high water levels for up to seven months during the non-flood period; benthic abundance increased continuously. When the gate was opened to drain, waterbirds from the nearby estuary quickly gathered to feed, accounting for about 6% of total estuary waterbirds. The experiment demonstrated that gate operation can increase habitat abundance. Galib et al. (2018) studied three sluice gates on the Baral River and found that partial opening significantly reduced downstream fish abundance relative to upstream; full opening made abundances similar. They recommended keeping at least one gate normally open (provided water level stayed below the warning line) to maintain longitudinal connectivity. Kuo and Wang (2018) performed a 5.5-hour inflow operation at the Budai salt-pan wetland in Chiayi; approximately 12,600 m³ of water entered, water levels rose markedly in some pans, and both climate-regulation and biodiversity ecosystem services improved. Wang et al. (2020) conducted three short-duration gate operations at the same site and showed that natural water inflow can create diverse depth and salinity conditions without increasing flood risk to the surrounding community.
Rocha等人（2016）於葡萄牙塔霍河口鹽田魚塭進行水門實驗，非汛期維持高水位達七個月，底棲生物豐度持續增加；排水後鄰近河口約6%的水鳥迅速聚集覓食，顯示水門操作可增加棲地豐度。Galib等人（2018）研究Baral河三座水門，發現部分開啟會使下游魚類豐度顯著低於上游，全開則上下游相近，建議在水位未超警戒下至少保持一門常開以維持縱向連通。Kuo與Wang（2018）於嘉義布袋鹽田進行5.5小時引水操作，約12,600 m³水進入，部分鹽田水位明顯上升，氣候調節與生物多樣性兩項生態系統服務皆獲提升。Wang等人（2020）於同地進行三次短時操作，顯示自然引水可創造多樣水深與鹽度條件，且不增加周邊社區淹水風險。
2.4 Application of the PHD Model　PHD模式的應用
The Physiographic Drainage-Inundation (PHD) Model is a quasi-two-dimensional model that combines the Cunge method with GIS. It is suitable for simulating inundation and drainage processes over complex topography such as salt pans, fish ponds and low-lying villages. Previous applications in Taiwan have successfully reproduced water-level changes under gate operations and rainfall events in coastal wetland systems, providing a practical tool for evaluating both habitat and flood-risk management strategies.
地文性淹排水模式（PHD Model）是結合Cunge法與GIS的準二維模式，適合模擬鹽田、魚塭與低窪村落等複雜地形的淹水與排水過程。先前在台灣的應用已成功重現沿海濕地系統在水門操作與降雨事件下的水位變化，為同時評估棲地與淹水風險管理策略提供實用工具。
2.5 Summary of Literature Review　文獻回顧摘要
The literature shows that water-gate operation can effectively improve the habitat environment of abandoned salt pans and turn these anthropogenic wetlands into alternative habitats for various species. Therefore this study applies the PHD Model to explore which gate-operation strategies can improve salt-pan habitat quality while still satisfying the flood-prevention needs of surrounding villages, and simultaneously assesses the impact of engineering land-use changes on gate operation.
文獻顯示，水門操作能有效改善廢曬鹽田的棲地環境，使這些人為濕地成為多種物種的替代棲地。因此本研究應用PHD模式探討哪些水門操作策略能在提升鹽田棲地品質的同時，仍滿足周邊村落的防洪需求，並同步評估工程改變土地利用對水門操作的影響。
Chapter 3 Research Area and Methods　第3章 研究區與方法
3.1 Study Area　研究區
The study area is the Taiqu (台區) salt pan within the Qigu Salt Pan Wetland, Tainan. It is bounded by the Xiashan Stream, Qigu Lagoon, roads and dikes, and contains a network of salt pans, a detention pond, culverts and water gates. The area is representative of the hydrological and management challenges faced by abandoned salt pans on the southwestern coast of Taiwan.
研究區為台南七股鹽田濕地內的台區鹽田，以下山溪、七股潟湖、道路與堤防為界，包含鹽田網絡、蓄水池、涵洞與水門。此區域能代表台灣西南沿海廢曬鹽田所面臨的水文與管理挑戰。

3.2 Facilities and Current Gate Operation　設施與現況水門操作
Key hydraulic facilities include Culvert 1 (connecting the detention pond), Culvert 2 (near area A5, often silted), Gate No.3 (main drainage gate toward the lagoon), Gate No.18 (main inflow gate from the Xiashan Stream) and Gate No.44 (used to isolate the village during heavy rain). Current practice typically uses Gate No.3 for low-tide drainage and Gate No.18 for monthly high-tide inflow. A mound between pan H and the village blocks backflow under normal conditions.
主要水利設施包括涵洞1（連接蓄水池）、涵洞2（近A5區，常淤積）、水門No.3（通往潟湖的主要排水門）、水門No.18（自下山溪的主要引水門）及水門No.44（豪雨時隔離村落）。現況實務通常以No.3於低潮排水、No.18於高潮引水。H區與村落間的擋土在常態下可阻擋回流。

3.3 Physiographic Drainage-Inundation (PHD) Model　地文性淹排水模式
The PHD Model is a quasi-two-dimensional hydrodynamic model that discretizes the study area into irregular computational cells based on topography and land-use. Flow between adjacent cells is calculated by a simplified form of the Saint-Venant equations (continuity and momentum) using the Cunge method, which is computationally efficient for large, low-gradient wetland systems. Boundary conditions include tidal levels at the lagoon and stream, rainfall hyetographs, and gate opening/closing schedules. The model is calibrated and verified against observed water-depth and flooding data.
PHD模式為準二維水動力模式，依地形與土地利用將研究區離散為不規則計算單元。相鄰單元間的流量以簡化聖維南方程式（連續與動量）搭配Cunge法計算，對大範圍低坡度濕地系統具有計算效率。邊界條件包括潟湖與溪流潮位、降雨歷線及水門啟閉時程。模式以實測水深與淹水資料進行校正與驗證。
3.4 Scenario Design　情境設計
Two inflow scenarios (Culvert 1 closed vs. open), six monthly drainage scenarios based on tidal characteristics, and rainfall events of multiple return periods (2- to 200-year) were designed. Engineering scenarios comprise (1) dredging of Culvert 2, (2) removal of the mound between H and the village, and (3) adjustment of salt-pan elevations. Habitat quality is evaluated by water-area increase and the distribution of preferred depth ranges for migratory birds; flood risk is evaluated by ponding depth and duration in Yancheng Village.
設計兩種引水情境（涵洞1關閉／開啟）、依潮汐特性區分的六種月別排水情境，以及多種重現期（2年至200年）降雨事件。工程情境包含（1）疏通涵洞2、（2）移除H與村落間擋土、（3）調整鹽田高程。棲地品質以水域面積增加及候鳥偏好水深範圍的分布評估；淹水風險以鹽埕村積水水深與歷時評估。
3.5 Simulation Assumptions　模擬假設
Key assumptions include: the study area is treated as completely closed (no overflow across dikes/roads); only rainfall, tide, elevation and water-depth changes are considered (evaporation, infiltration, temperature and air pressure are neglected); three pumps operate continuously during rainfall simulations according to local practice; and a 24-hour recession period with zero rainfall is applied after each design storm.
主要假設包括：研究區視為完全封閉（堤防／道路無溢流）；僅考慮降雨、潮位、高程與水深變化（忽略蒸發、入滲、溫度與氣壓）；降雨模擬期間依地方操作習慣三部抽水機持續運轉；每次設計暴雨後設定24小時退水期且降雨為0 mm。
Chapter 4 Results and Discussion　第4章 結果與討論
4.1 Data Collection and Survey Results　資料蒐集與調查結果
4.1.1 Water Depth Survey Results　水深調查結果
From Figure 4.1, the water depth at the five sample points was completely dry in January and gradually rose after entering the flood period in May. With the end of the flood period in September, in October and December (except for salt pan W4) the salt pans had basically returned to dryness. This showed that when there was insufficient rainfall in the non-flood period, the salt pans were mostly dry and barren due to strong environmental evaporation and poor connection to external water resources. The non-flood period from October to March of the following year is also the season for migratory birds to winter in Qigu District, with peak numbers from November to February (Chen et al., 2020). Bird abundance is related to habitat water depth to a certain extent (Ma et al., 2010; Barnagaud et al., 2019). Therefore the lack of effective water-inflow operation during the non-flood period left the salt pans in a prolonged dry state that was not conducive to bird use.
由圖4.1可知，五個測點的水深在1月完全乾涸，進入5月汛期後逐漸上升。汛期於9月結束後，10月與12月除W4鹽田外，其餘鹽田大致已恢復乾涸狀態。這顯示非汛期若無充足降雨，鹽田因環境蒸發強烈且外部水源連通性差，多呈乾旱裸露。非汛期（10月至翌年3月）同時也是候鳥至七股地區度冬的季節，鳥類數量高峰出現於11月至翌年2月（Chen et al., 2020）。鳥類豐度在某種程度上與棲地水深相關（Ma et al., 2010；Barnagaud et al., 2019）。因此，非汛期缺乏有效的引水操作，導致鹽田長期處於乾旱狀態，此種棲地環境不利於鳥類利用。
4.1.2 Tide Level Survey Results　潮位調查結果
In order to understand the tidal-level relationship among the Qigu Lagoon outside Gate No.3, the Xiashan Stream outside Gate No.18 and the Jiangjun observation station, HOBO self-recording water-level gauges were placed outside Gates No.3 and No.18 for comparison with the Jiangjun station data. Analysis showed that the R² between the Jiangjun station and the Qigu Lagoon was 0.9597, with the conversion equation y = 0.2418x² + 0.4637x + 0.0807. The R² between the Jiangjun station and the Xiashan Stream was 0.7865, with the equation y = 0.2957x² + 0.2889x + 0.5446. From the monthly average marigram of the Qigu Lagoon after conversion, the month with the lowest mean water was January, and the highest were August and September. The tide type in the study area is a mixed tide, with two high and two low waters of unequal height in one day. The tide levels outside both Gate No.3 and Gate No.18 lagged the Jiangjun station by about one hour, and the tidal range in the lagoon and stream was smaller than that at Jiangjun.
為瞭解水門No.3外側七股潟湖、水門No.18外側下山溪與將軍觀測站之間的潮位關係，於No.3與No.18外側設置HOBO自記水位計，與將軍站資料進行比較。分析顯示將軍站與七股潟湖的R²為0.9597，換算式為 y = 0.2418x² + 0.4637x + 0.0807；將軍站與下山溪的R²為0.7865，換算式為 y = 0.2957x² + 0.2889x + 0.5446。由換算後七股潟湖月平均潮位圖可知，平均水位最低月份為1月，最高為8月與9月。研究區潮型為混合潮，一日內出現不等高的兩次高潮與低潮。No.3與No.18外側潮位皆較將軍站延遲約1小時，且潟湖與溪流的潮差小於將軍站。
4.1.3 Elevation of the Study Area　研究區高程
Elevation data of the Taiqu salt pans were obtained from existing topographic surveys and field measurements. The overall elevation of the salt pans is lower than the mean water level of the Xiashan Stream outside Gate No.18, which explains why Gate No.18 can only function as an inflow gate and cannot be used for drainage. Local high points (mounds) and the relative elevation between the detention pond and the salt pans control the direction and speed of water movement once a gate is opened.
台區鹽田高程資料來自既有地形測量與現地量測。鹽田整體高程低於No.18外側下山溪平均水位，這解釋了為何No.18只能作為引水門而無法用於排水。局部高點（擋土）以及蓄水池與鹽田之間的相對高程，控制了水門開啟後水流的方向與速度。
4.2 Water Gate Inflow Operation　水門引水操作
After long-term coordination with Taijiang National Park and local residents, water-inflow operations began on 20 October 2020 and were carried out every two weeks, each lasting about 4 hours. This study selected the operations on 20 October 2020 and 16 March 2021 to record hourly water-depth changes. Culvert 1 was closed during these operations so that incoming water would flow into the southern salt pans more rapidly instead of entering the western detention pond.
經與台江國家公園及在地居民長期協調後，自2020年10月20日起每兩週進行一次引水操作，每次約4小時。本研究選取2020年10月20日與2021年3月16日兩次操作，逐時記錄鹽田水深變化。操作期間封閉涵洞1，使進水能較快流入南側鹽田，而非進入西側蓄水池。
4.2.1 Gate Operation in October 2020　2020年10月水門操作
On 20 October 2020 Gate No.18 was opened at 12:00 and closed at 16:00 (approximately 4 hours). The highest tide of the day occurred at 14:00 with a level of 1.119 m (annual mean highest water of the Xiashan Stream = 1.055 m). Before the gate was opened, except for the channel, B5 and H, the other salt-pan areas had no water; the safety gauge, B5 and H recorded 35 cm, 5 cm and 25 cm respectively. In the first hour water flowed quickly into areas close to the channel (A1, B1, C1, D1, G). After 4 hours the areas with water were A1–A3, B1–B3, C1–C2, the first half of E1, G, and the originally wet B5 and H. The highest depth was 63 cm at H; depths greater than 10 cm occurred at B1, B2, D1–D3, E1 and G. The safety-gauge depth rose to 52 cm. Because the channel was blocked by a mound, water could not flow southward through the channel; most water first entered the channel connected to Culvert 1 and then spread into the pans. Even though the salt fields had been dredged and the selected tide was higher than the mean, the 4-hour inflow volume was still insufficient to wet all pans.
2020年10月20日於12:00開啟水門No.18，16:00關閉（約4小時）。當日最高潮位出現於14:00，為1.119 m（下山溪年平均最高水位為1.055 m）。開啟前除渠道、B5與H外，其餘鹽田無水；安全測點、B5、H水深分別為35 cm、5 cm、25 cm。第1小時水迅速流入靠近渠道的A1、B1、C1、D1、G等區。4小時後有水區域為A1–A3、B1–B3、C1–C2、E1前半、G，以及原本有水的B5與H。最高水深為H的63 cm；B1、B2、D1–D3、E1、G水深超過10 cm。安全測點水深升至52 cm。因渠道被擋土阻擋，水無法經渠道向南流動，多數水先進入連接涵洞1的渠道再擴散至鹽田。即使鹽田已疏浚且選用的潮位高於平均，4小時進水量仍不足以使所有鹽田進水。
4.2.2 Gate Operation in March 2021　2021年3月水門操作
The operation on 16 March 2021 opened the gate at 11:40 and closed it at 15:40 (about 4 hours). The highest water level of the day was 0.935 m at 13:40. Water-depth changes before and after the operation followed a similar spatial pattern to the October event, with rapid filling of near-channel cells and slower propagation into more distant pans. Comparison of the two events confirmed that the presence of the mound and the status of Culvert 1 strongly control the spatial distribution of inflow.
2021年3月16日操作於11:40開啟水門、15:40關閉（約4小時）。當日最高水位0.935 m出現於13:40。操作前後水深變化的空間模式與10月事件相似，靠近渠道的單元迅速進水，較遠鹽田擴散較慢。兩次事件的比較確認，擋土的存在與涵洞1的狀態強烈控制引水的空間分布。
4.3 Model Setting and Verification　模式設定與驗證
The computational grid was drawn according to the topography, land-use and the locations of channels, culverts and gates. Model verification used the observed water-depth data from the October 2020 and March 2021 gate operations. Root-mean-square error (RMSE) and the Nash–Sutcliffe efficiency (NSE) were calculated for the safety gauge and for the whole salt-pan area. For the October operation the RMSE was 0.0494 m; the simulated and observed water-depth trends were close, indicating that the PHD model possesses a satisfactory degree of accuracy for the subsequent scenario simulations. Rainfall-event verification against the 0823 (2018) event also showed that the model reproduced the timing and approximate magnitude of village ponding (observed peak ~30–40 cm, simulated peak ~29.5 cm).
計算網格依地形、土地利用以及渠道、涵洞與水門位置劃分。模式驗證採用2020年10月與2021年3月水門操作的實測水深資料，對安全測點與整個鹽田區域計算均方根誤差（RMSE）與Nash–Sutcliffe效率係數（NSE）。10月操作的RMSE為0.0494 m，模擬與觀測的水深變化趨勢接近，顯示PHD模式具有足夠精度可供後續情境模擬使用。針對2018年0823降雨事件的驗證也顯示，模式能重現村落積水的時間與大致幅度（觀測峰值約30–40 cm，模擬峰值約29.5 cm）。
4.4 Frequency Analysis Results　頻率分析結果
Rainfall duration for the frequency analysis was selected as 48 hours. Maximum 48-hour rainfall data from 1993 to 2020 at the Qigu station were collected and analysed with five probability distributions (PT3, LPT3, LN2, LN3, EV1). Parameters of each distribution were obtained with EasyFit. Goodness-of-fit tests (K-S, A-D, Chi-squared) at the 95 % confidence level showed that only the Extreme-Value Type I (EV1) distribution passed all three tests; it was therefore adopted for the design hyetographs of the 2-, 5-, 10-, 25-, 50-, 100- and 200-year return periods used in the subsequent rainfall scenarios.
頻率分析選用的降雨延時為48小時。蒐集七股站1993–2020年最大48小時降雨資料，並以五種機率分布（PT3、LPT3、LN2、LN3、EV1）進行分析，參數由EasyFit求得。在95%信心水準下的適合度檢定（K-S、A-D、卡方）顯示，僅極值I型（EV1）分布通過全部三項檢定，因此採用EV1作為後續2、5、10、25、50、100、200年重現期設計雨型的分布。
4.5 Simulation Results of Each Scenario　各情境模擬結果
To establish a long-term operation strategy based on current conditions, the timing and requirements of gate operation were divided into three categories: (1) non-flood-period inflow operation, (2) drainage operation on days without rainfall, and (3) village flooding condition during rainfall events. The simulation results for each scenario are described in detail below.
為因應不同操作目的並建立基於現況的長期操作策略，本研究將水門操作的時機與需求分為三種條件：第一種為非汛期引水操作，第二種為無降雨事件時的排水操作，第三種為降雨事件期間村落的淹水情形。以下依各操作情境的模擬結果進行詳細說明。
4.5.1.1 Water Inflow Operation　引水操作
The non-flood period in this study area was from October to April of the following year. This period was also the season for migratory birds to migrate and overwinter. The salt pans in the study area were almost dry and barren during non-flood period, and this environment was not conducive to the use of organisms. Therefore, the main purpose of the water inflow operation was to use the gate No.18 to let the water from the Xiashan stream flow into the salt pans for natural water exchange, and at the same time create the opportunity to bring in various nutrient sources, food sources, increasing habitat types of different water depths, and water area to improve the quality of the habitat in the salt pans for the use of migratory birds.
本研究區非汛期為10月至翌年4月。此時期同時也是候鳥遷徙與度冬的季節。非汛期研究區鹽田幾乎呈乾旱裸露，不利於生物利用。因此，引水操作的主要目的是利用水門No.18讓下山溪的水流入鹽田進行自然水交換，同時創造帶入各種營養源、食物源的機會，增加不同水深的棲地類型與水域面積，以提升鹽田棲地品質供候鳥使用。
In order to explore the influence of different water inflow operation methods on the water depth in the salt pans, this study divided the water inflow operation into two scenarios. Scenario A used a board to block Culvert 1, cutting the connection between the salt pans and the detention pond, so that incoming water could flow to the southern part of the salt pan more quickly. Scenario B opened the board of Culvert 1 to allow water to flow naturally according to different elevations. Both scenarios were combined with 24-hour operation duration to observe the water depth and water area changes in the salt pans, and to select a better inflow operation scenario under the premise of not exceeding the warning water depth.
為探討不同引水操作方式對鹽田水深的影響，本研究將引水操作分為兩種情境。情境A以檔板封閉涵洞1，切斷鹽田與蓄水池的連通，使進水能較快速流向鹽田南部。情境B開啟涵洞1檔板，讓水依不同高程自然流動。兩種情境皆搭配24小時操作時程，觀察鹽田水深與水域面積變化，並在不超過警戒水深的前提下選擇較佳的引水操作情境。
Scenario A (Culvert 1 closed): From the results of Figure 4.18, under the condition that Culvert 1 was closed, the water inflow from the water gate No.18 quickly spread below the salt pan after 4 hours. After 8 hours of water inflow, except for the salt pans D5 and E4 which had no water, the water depth in other areas reached 1 to 15 cm. At the same time, part of the water would flow into the detention pond through Culvert 2 of A5. After 24 hours of inflow, the entire salt pan had a certain depth of water, and the total water area in the salt pan reached the best. The total water area in the salt pans increased from 13,703.39 m² before the water inflow to 201,481.93 m², a total water area increase of 187,778.54 m². In terms of habitat type changes, most of the salt pans were dry and barren before the inflow operation; after 24 hours of operation, the type of waters with a depth of 5 to 10 cm increased from the original 0 blocks to 5 blocks, and the type of water area of 10 to 20 cm increased from 0 blocks to 15 blocks. The main habitat type changed from the original bare soil to a shallow water environment with a water depth of 10 to 20 cm (Table 4.9 / 4.10).
情境A（涵洞1封閉）：由圖4.18結果可知，在涵洞1封閉條件下，水門No.18的進水於4小時後迅速擴散至鹽田下方。引水8小時後，除D5與E4鹽田無水外，其他區域水深達1至15 cm。同時部分水會經由A5的涵洞2流入蓄水池。引水24小時後，整個鹽田皆有一定水深，總水域面積達到最佳。鹽田總水域面積由引水前的13,703.39 m²增加至201,481.93 m²，共增加187,778.54 m²。就棲地類型變化而言，引水操作前多數鹽田為乾旱裸露；操作24小時後，水深5–10 cm的水域類型由原來的0個區塊增加至5個區塊，水深10–20 cm的水域類型由0個區塊增加至15個區塊。主要棲地類型由原本的裸地轉變為水深10–20 cm的淺水環境（表4.9 / 4.10）。
A phenomenon could be observed that although the tide level outside the gate No.18 began to decrease in the 2nd hour after the operation and reached the lowest water level at the 7th hour, the water outside the gate still continued to flow into the salt pans. This showed that even if the tide level outside the gate No.18 was at the lowest water level, its water level was still higher than the water level in the salt pan, so water could only be incoming but not draining.
可觀察到一現象：雖然操作後第2小時起No.18外側潮位開始下降，並於第7小時達最低水位，但外側水仍持續流入鹽田。這顯示即使No.18外側潮位處於最低，其水位仍高於鹽田水位，因此水只能進而不能排。
Scenario B (Culvert 1 open): When Culvert 1 was open, the total water area in the salt pans increased from 13,703.39 m² to 143,631.09 m², but the increase in area was less than that of Scenario A. The overall performance in creating diverse shallow-water habitats was also lower than that of Scenario A.
情境B（涵洞1開啟）：當涵洞1開啟時，鹽田總水域面積由13,703.39 m²增加至143,631.09 m²，但面積增加量少於情境A。在創造多樣淺水棲地方面的整體表現也低於情境A。
4.5.1.2 Draining Operation　排水操作
From the simulation results of draining operation (Figure 4.22), in January the water depth of the No.3 gauge started at 0.384 m. After 1 hour of continuous operation (t1), it reached the lowest water depth of 0.21 m during the draining period, with a decrease of about 17.4 cm. After about 2 hours, the water depth increased to 0.266 m due to backflow from the lagoon (increase of 5.6 cm). At the 3rd hour (t3), it rose further to 0.36 m (increase of 9.4 cm). At this time the water depth of the detention pond rose significantly.
由排水操作模擬結果（圖4.22）可知，1月No.3測點初始水深為0.384 m。連續操作1小時（t1）後達排水期間最低水深0.21 m，下降約17.4 cm。約2小時後因潟湖回流，水深上升至0.266 m（增加5.6 cm）。第3小時（t3）進一步上升至0.36 m（增加9.4 cm）。此時蓄水池水深明顯上升。
The same trend was observed in February (Figure 4.23(b)). One hour after operation, the No.3 gauge reached the lowest depth of 0.24 m, then rose to 0.303 m at the 2nd hour and to 0.392 m at the 3rd hour. The main difference between January and February was that the lagoon tide level in February was higher than in January, resulting in earlier and stronger backflow influence.
2月也呈現相同趨勢（圖4.23(b)）。操作1小時後No.3測點達最低水深0.24 m，第2小時升至0.303 m，第3小時升至0.392 m。1月與2月最大差異在於2月潟湖潮位高於1月，導致回流影響更早且更明顯。
In March and May the changing trend of water depth in the salt pans and detention pond was similar to that in January and February. When the water gate was opened 1 hour before the lowest tide level, the water in the detention pond drained into the lagoon effectively in the first 1–1.5 hours. After interviews with the water gate operator, it was learned that the mean tide level of the Qigu Lagoon was the highest in summer, which also meant that summer was not conducive to draining.
3月與5月鹽田與蓄水池水深變化趨勢與1、2月相似。水門於最低潮位前1小時開啟時，蓄水池在最初1–1.5小時內可有效排入潟湖。經水門操作員訪談得知，七股潟湖平均潮位以夏季最高，代表夏季較不利排水。
Simulation results for July and August (Figure 4.26) showed that during draining operations the water depth of the detention pond decreased only slightly after the water gate was opened for 1 hour; in August there was almost no change. As the tide level rose, water from the lagoon quickly flowed back into the detention pond and then into the salt pans after about 2 hours. Because the tide level in July and August was the highest of the year, the depth of water in the detention pond and salt pans also increased the most after the backflow.
7月與8月模擬結果（圖4.26）顯示，排水操作開啟水門1小時後蓄水池水深僅略微下降，8月幾乎無變化。隨著潮位上升，潟湖水迅速回流至蓄水池，約2小時後流入鹽田。因7、8月潮位為全年最高，回流後蓄水池與鹽田水深增加幅度也最大。
Overall, during the non-flood period the effective draining window is about 1.5–2 hours before significant backflow occurs. During the flood period the window is shorter (about 1–1.5 hours) because of higher lagoon tide. The water depths of the detention pond and salt pans in the non-flood period decreased by 1.4–8.7 cm and 1.3–2.6 cm respectively; during the flood period the decreases were smaller (0.5–3.1 cm and 1–1.8 cm). January is the easiest month for draining; August is the most difficult.
整體而言，非汛期有效排水窗口約1.5–2小時（之後回流顯著）；汛期因潟湖潮位較高，窗口較短（約1–1.5小時）。非汛期蓄水池與鹽田水深分別下降1.4–8.7 cm與1.3–2.6 cm；汛期下降幅度較小（0.5–3.1 cm與1–1.8 cm）。1月為最易排水月份，8月則最難。
4.5.1.3 Village Flooding Condition after Rainfall Event　降雨事件後村落淹水情形
Under design rainfall events of various return periods (2, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100, 200 years), the model simulated ponding in Yancheng Village. For the 2-year return period, ponding appeared around the 32nd hour after the rainfall peak and reached a maximum depth of about 1 cm at the 36th hour. Under higher return periods the ponding depth and duration increased. With three pumps in operation and increased salt-pan detention capacity, the study area is able to handle rainfall events of less than the 200-year return period without causing severe flooding in the surrounding villages. Long-term inflow operation does not increase the flood risk to nearby communities.
在不同重現期（2、5、10、25、50、100、200年）設計降雨事件下，模式模擬了鹽程村的積水情形。2年重現期時，積水約於雨峰後第32小時出現，第36小時達最深約1 cm。重現期越高，積水深與持續時間越長。在設置3部抽水機並增加鹽田滯洪容量後，研究區足以因應小於200年重現期的降雨事件，不致造成周邊村落嚴重淹水。長時間引水操作不會增加鄰近社區的淹水風險。
4.5.2 Engineering Scenario Simulation Results　工程情境模擬結果
Three engineering measures were examined: (1) dredging of Culvert 2, (2) removal of the mound between area H and the village, and (3) elevation adjustment of selected salt-pan cells. These were combined with the preferred gate-operation strategies.
本研究檢視三種工程措施：（1）疏通涵洞2、（2）移除H區與村落間的擋土、（3）調整選定鹽田格網高程，並與較佳水門操作策略結合。
Culvert dredging improved local water circulation within the salt pans and allowed slightly more efficient water exchange during both inflow and draining operations. Removal of the mound between H and the village produced almost no change in water-depth distribution under any of the tested scenarios, indicating that the mound does not currently act as a primary hydraulic control for the simulated conditions. Elevation adjustment increased the detention capacity of the salt pans under moderate rainfall events and helped create more favorable depth ranges for habitat. However, under extreme (200-year) rainfall the benefit of elevation change became almost negligible.
涵洞疏通改善了鹽田內部局部水循環，使引水與排水操作時的水交換略為順暢。移除H與村落間擋土在所有測試情境下對水深分布幾乎無影響，顯示在模擬條件下該擋土並非主要水力控制因素。高程調整可在中等降雨事件下增加鹽田滯洪容量，並有助於創造較佳棲地水深範圍。然而，在極端（200年）降雨下，高程改變的效益幾乎可忽略。
Overall, engineering measures can improve water connectivity and help achieve desired habitat depth distributions when combined with appropriate gate operations. Their contribution to reducing flood risk under extreme rainfall remains limited; therefore they should be regarded as complementary rather than primary flood-mitigation measures.
整體而言，工程措施與適當水門操作結合後，可改善水域連通性並有助於達成目標棲地水深分布。其對極端降雨下降低淹水風險的貢獻仍有限，因此應視為輔助措施而非主要防洪手段。

Chapter 5 Conclusion and Suggestion　第5章 結論與建議
5.1 Conclusion　結論
5.1.1 Current Condition　現況
The Qigu salt-pan wetland, after the cessation of salt production, has become an important alternative habitat for many organisms. However, the closed design inherited from the salt-making period, combined with insufficient management, often results in excessively low water levels during the non-flood season, leading to poor water quality and high salinity that limit biological use of the habitat.
七股鹽田濕地在廢曬後已成為許多生物的重要替代棲地。然而，承襲自曬鹽時期的封閉式設計，加上管理不足，非汛期常出現水位過低、水質不佳與鹽度過高等問題，限制生物利用這些人為棲地的可能性。
5.1.2 Operation Scenario　操作情境
1. From the inflow operation results, water inflow can significantly increase the water area of the study site and the preferred habitat types of migratory birds, effectively enhancing habitat diversity. Closing Culvert 1 yields a larger water area. However, if the goal is to improve habitat quality without exceeding the warning water depth, keeping Culvert 1 open and maintaining Gate No.18 normally open during the non-flood season so that the salt pans behave more like an intertidal wetland is the preferable inflow strategy.
2. From the draining scenario results, the effective draining duration is longer in the non-flood season (about 1.5–2 hours) and shorter in the flood season (about 1–1.5 hours). The decrease in water depth of the detention pond and salt pans is greater in the non-flood season. This study recommends that in the non-flood season, draining should be performed a few days before forecasted rainfall according to CWA forecasts so that water depth is lowered to a safe range; in the flood season, because lagoon tide levels are high and draining is difficult, frequent short operations on non-rainy days are advised to maintain safe water depths.
3. Rainfall simulation results show that with three pumps installed and increased salt-pan detention capacity, the present study area is able to cope with rainfall events of less than the 200-year return period. At the same time, inflow operation does not increase the flood risk of surrounding villages.
1. 由引水操作結果可知，引水可顯著增加研究區水域面積與候鳥偏好棲地類型，並有效提升棲地多樣性。封閉涵洞1可獲得較大水域面積。但若以提升棲地品質且不超過警戒水深為目標，則保持涵洞1開啟、非汛期維持No.18常開，使鹽田更接近潮間帶濕地，是較佳的引水操作方式。
2. 由排水情境結果可知，非汛期有效排水時間較長（約1.5–2小時），汛期較短（約1–1.5小時）。非汛期蓄水池與鹽田水深下降幅度較大。本研究建議非汛期依據中央氣象署降雨預報，於降雨事件前數日進行排水，使水深降至安全範圍；汛期因潟湖潮位較高、排水不易，建議在無雨日頻繁操作，以維持安全水深。
3. 降雨模擬結果顯示，在設置3部抽水機並增加鹽田滯洪容量後，現況研究區足以因應小於200年重現期的降雨事件。同時，引水操作不會增加周邊村落的淹水風險。
5.1.3 Engineering Scenario　工程情境
Engineering measures (culvert dredging, mound removal, elevation change) can improve water connectivity and help achieve desired habitat depth distributions when combined with gate operations. However, their effect on reducing flood risk under extreme (200-year) rainfall is limited. Elevation adjustment can provide a modest increase in detention capacity under moderate rainfall, but the benefit is almost negligible under high-intensity events.
工程措施（涵洞疏通、擋土移除、高程調整）與水門操作結合後，可改善水域連通性並有助於達成目標棲地水深分布。然而，其對極端（200年）降雨下降低淹水風險的效果有限。高程調整可在中等降雨下適度增加滯洪容量，但在高強度事件下效益幾乎可忽略。
5.2 Suggestion　建議
Based on the scenario simulation results of inflow and drainage, this study formulated the following water-gate operation strategies: During non-flood seasons, open Culvert 1 so that the detention pond is included in the operating range. When the water level in the salt pans and the tide level of the Xiashan Stream reach equilibrium, the salt pans will behave like an intertidal wetland, increasing both water area and habitat diversity without exceeding the warning water depth. Maintain Gate No.18 in a normally open state during the non-flood period for continuous gentle water exchange. Perform proactive draining a few days before forecasted rainfall in the non-flood season, and use more frequent short draining operations on non-rainy days during the flood season. Engineering improvements (especially selective elevation adjustment and ensuring culvert capacity) can be considered as complementary measures, but should not be relied upon as the sole solution for extreme rainfall events.
根據引水與排水情境模擬結果，本研究提出以下水門操作策略：非汛期開啟涵洞1，使蓄水池納入操作範圍。當鹽田水位與下山溪潮位達平衡時，鹽田將呈現類似潮間帶濕地的狀態，可增加水域面積與棲地多樣性且不超過警戒水深。非汛期維持No.18常開，進行持續溫和的水交換。非汛期依預報於降雨前數日主動排水；汛期則在無雨日以較頻繁的短時排水維持安全水深。工程改善（尤其是選擇性高程調整與確保涵洞通暢）可作為輔助措施，但不應作為極端降雨事件的唯一對策。
